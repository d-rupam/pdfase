About the Redaction Tool

Redaction is a critical security measure used in legal discovery, medical record sharing (HIPAA compliance), and government FOIA releases to protect personally identifiable information (PII). The PDFase Redact PDF tool is engineered specifically to give you professional-grade sanitization tools without the massive privacy risks of web-based cloud editors.

Because all logic is executed directly inside your web browser via client-side JavaScript, your raw, un-redacted documents are never exposed to external servers. You maintain absolute control over the digital chain of custody.

Why Name it PDFase?

In biology, an enzyme (which usually ends in "-ase") is a tiny, high-speed machine that attaches to raw materials to cut, build, or change them instantly. We applied this exact same idea to our digital tools. PDFase acts like a fast digital enzyme for your documents—modifying your files locally, instantly, and securely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my files uploaded to a server when redacting?

No. The Redact PDF tool processes all documents locally inside your web browser. Absolute data sovereignty is maintained, ensuring your highly sensitive files never leave your device.

Is the redacted text completely unrecoverable?

Yes. Proper redaction is not just a visual black box. Once you apply the changes and download, the underlying text and metadata beneath the box are sanitized, making it impossible to highlight, copy, or search for the hidden information.

Can I redact images and specific areas?

Yes, you can click and drag redaction boxes anywhere on the page to obscure text paragraphs, signatures, photos, or any other sensitive visual element.

Does this tool work offline?

Yes. Once the web page is fully loaded in your browser, the tool operates entirely offline without needing an active internet connection, perfect for secure, air-gapped workstations.
