Step-by-Step Guide to Scanning Locally

Digitizing your physical paperwork is highly secure and straightforward. Follow these steps:

  • Enable Camera: Click the 'Enable Camera' button and grant your browser permission to access your device's video feed.
  • Align & Capture: Hold your document steady in the frame and click the circular capture button to take a snapshot.
  • Review Pages: Capture additional pages as needed. They will appear in a grid layout below the scanner.
  • Generate PDF: Click 'Create PDF' to instantly bind your captured images into a standardized document format.

About the Scan to PDF Tool

Traditional mobile scanning apps are notorious for collecting user data, requiring accounts, or uploading your highly sensitive financial and medical documents to third-party cloud processors. The PDFase Scan to PDF tool is engineered to bypass servers entirely.

By leveraging native HTML5 WebRTC APIs, we pipe your camera feed directly into a local canvas element. When you finalize your document, client-side cryptographic and layout engines compile the PDF on your device, ensuring maximum privacy.

Why Name it PDFase?

In biology, an enzyme (which usually ends in "-ase") is a tiny, high-speed machine that attaches to raw materials to cut, build, or change them instantly. We applied this exact same idea to our digital tools. PDFase acts like a fast digital enzyme for your documents—modifying your files locally, instantly, and securely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my camera pictures sent to a server?

No. The Scan to PDF tool accesses your camera and processes the images entirely inside your web browser. Your captured documents never leave your device.

Can I capture multiple pages for a single PDF?

Yes. You can take as many pictures as you need. They will be added to a local gallery interface where you can compile them into one multi-page PDF document.

Does this tool work on mobile devices?

Absolutely. It uses standard web camera APIs, allowing you to use your smartphone or tablet's rear camera to scan documents directly into the mobile browser.

What if my scan looks blurry?

Ensure you are in a well-lit environment and tap your screen (on mobile) to allow the camera to auto-focus before clicking the capture button.
