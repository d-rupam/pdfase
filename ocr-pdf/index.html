About the OCR PDF Tool

Most free online OCR (Optical Character Recognition) services force you to upload your sensitive tax returns, IDs, or confidential contracts to an unknown server. The PDFase OCR tool is engineered to solve this by moving the machine learning engine directly to your device.

By compiling powerful OCR libraries into WebAssembly (WASM), your browser is capable of scanning the pixels of your document and recreating the text layer over the images natively. You retain total control of your data from start to finish.

Why Name it PDFase?

In biology, an enzyme (which usually ends in "-ase") is a tiny, high-speed machine that attaches to raw materials to cut, build, or change them instantly. We applied this exact same idea to our digital tools. PDFase acts like a fast digital enzyme for your documents—modifying your files locally, instantly, and securely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my scanned documents sent to a server?

No. The OCR tool uses WebAssembly to run the optical character recognition engine entirely inside your browser. Your private documents never leave your device.

What does OCR do exactly?

OCR converts a picture of text (like a scanned page) into actual digital text. This makes the document searchable using Ctrl+F, and allows you to copy and paste the words into other applications.

Is the text extraction 100% accurate?

Accuracy depends heavily on the quality of your original scan. High-resolution documents with clear, standard fonts will yield near-perfect results, while handwritten or blurry text may have errors.

Does this tool work offline?

Yes. Once the web application and the specific language data models are loaded into your browser cache, the tool processes the document offline.
