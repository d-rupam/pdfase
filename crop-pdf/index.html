About the Crop Tool

Scanned documents often come with massive, uneven white borders that look unprofessional and waste ink when printing. The PDFase Crop tool is engineered to fix this by letting you visually define the bounding box of your document.

Because all logic is executed directly inside your web browser via client-side JavaScript, your raw documents are never exposed to external servers. You maintain absolute control over your digital files while achieving perfectly trimmed results.

Why Name it PDFase?

In biology, an enzyme (which usually ends in "-ase") is a tiny, high-speed machine that attaches to raw materials to cut, build, or change them instantly. We applied this exact same idea to our digital tools. PDFase acts like a fast digital enzyme for your documents—modifying your files locally, instantly, and securely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server when cropping?

No. The Crop PDF tool processes all documents locally inside your web browser. Absolute data sovereignty is maintained, ensuring your files never leave your device.

How does the cropping tool work?

You visually draw a crop box over your document. The tool then adjusts the internal PDF bounding boxes (CropBox/MediaBox) to hide the outer margins permanently.

Does cropping reduce the file size?

Cropping primarily changes the visible dimensions of the page. It does not necessarily compress the underlying images or text, but it removes the unwanted margins from view when reading or printing.

Does this tool work offline?

Yes. Once the web page is fully loaded in your browser, the tool operates entirely offline without needing an active internet connection.
