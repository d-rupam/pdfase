Step-by-Step Guide to Password Protecting a PDF Locally

Our tool is designed to be simple and fast. Follow these easy steps to secure your document:

  • Select your file: Drag and drop your PDF into the upload box above, or click the 'Select file' button to choose it from your device.
  • Set your password: Type your own secret password, or use our built-in Password Generator to instantly create a highly secure, customizable cryptographic key.
  • Encrypt the PDF: Important: Use the convenient copy button to save your password somewhere safe first! Because we process everything locally, we cannot recover lost passwords. Once saved, click the 'Encrypt PDF' button to lock the document.
  • Download your file: Save your newly encrypted, secure PDF file directly to your device.

About the Add Password Tool

The PDFase Add Password tool is built to secure your important files against unauthorized access. Whether you are sending confidential financial records or personal agreements over email, locking the document ensures that only the intended recipient with the correct password can view it.

Because the encryption occurs completely inside your web browser, you never have to trust an external server with your sensitive passwords or confidential data files.

Why Name it PDFase?

In biology, an enzyme (which usually ends in "-ase") is a tiny, high-speed machine that attaches to raw materials to cut, build, or change them instantly. We applied this exact same idea to our digital tools. PDFase acts like a fast digital enzyme for your documents—modifying your files locally, instantly, and securely, without ever sending your private data over the internet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my passwords or PDF files sent to a server?

No. The Add Password tool processes encryption entirely inside your web browser. Your password and files never leave your device.

Can I remove the password later if I forget it?

For security reasons, if you lose or forget the password you set, neither our tool nor anyone else can recover it because processing is completely local.

Is the encryption secure?

Yes. We use robust cryptographic algorithms to lock your document so that it cannot be opened without the correct security code.

Does this tool work offline?

Yes. Once the web page is fully loaded in your browser, the tool operates entirely offline without needing an active internet connection.
