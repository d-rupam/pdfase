 How to Convert ChatGPT or Gemini Quizzes to Google Forms

                
Skip manual copy-pasting and paid Google Workspace add-ons. Generate an auto-graded Google Form directly inside your own Google account in under a minute:

                
    
                    
  • Generate or copy your quiz: Click Copy AI Prompt to ask ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude for MCQs (or copy any existing quiz text from Word or Google Docs).
    • 
                    
  • Paste & Extract: Paste the text into the box above and click Extract Questions. PDFase automatically detects options, marks correct answers, and loads explanations.
    • 
                    
  • Quick Review: Set your form title, tweak any question or feedback, and toggle Quiz Mode, Required, or Question Numbers as needed.
    • 
                    
  • Copy & Open Apps Script: Click 1. Copy Form Code, then click 2. Open Google Apps Script.
    • 
                    
  • Paste, Save & Run: Replace the default code in Google Apps Script with your copied script, press Ctrl+S to save, and click Run. On your first run, approve Google's permission prompt (Review Permissions → Choose account → Advanced → Go to Untitled project → Allow) and open the form links shown in the Execution Log.

 Why Name it PDFase?

                
In biology, an enzyme (which usually ends in "-ase") is a tiny, high-speed machine that attaches to raw materials to cut, build, or change them instantly. We applied this exact same idea to our digital tools. PDFase acts like a fast digital enzyme for your documents and quizzes—processing everything locally, instantly, and securely without ever sending your private data over the internet.

            


        


        
        

            
Frequently Asked Questions

            
            

                
 Can I paste directly from ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude without cleaning the text?

                
Yes. Just hit the copy button on your AI response and paste it straight in. PDFase automatically ignores intro lines (like "Sure! Here are 20 MCQs..."), strips bold Markdown stars, and removes sign-off fluff at the end.

            


            

                
 How does automatic Answer Key and Explanation detection work?

                
If your text has lines like Answer: B and Explanation: ... under each question—or a separate Answer Key / Solutions section at the bottom—PDFase matches them to the right question, marks the green answer tick, and adds the explanation as Google Forms quiz feedback.

            


            

                
 Why don't I have to sign in with Google on this site?

                
To protect your privacy and avoid API limits or paid add-on paywalls, PDFase never connects to your Google Drive. Instead, it generates a clean Google Apps Script that you run yourself inside Google's official Apps Script editor.

            


            

                
 Why does Google show an "Unverified App" warning when I click Run?

                
Google displays that screen whenever you run a new Apps Script in your own account that creates a Google Form. Because you pasted the script yourself inside your own Google account, click Advanced → Go to Untitled project → Allow to let it create your form.

            


            

                
 Is there any limit on the number of questions?

                
No. Unlike paid extensions that stop after 5 or 10 questions, PDFase lets you convert 50, 100, or more questions at once—completely free and unlimited.
