Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server when merging?

No. The Merge PDF tool processes all documents locally inside your web browser. Absolute data sovereignty is maintained, and your files never leave your device.

Is there a limit to how many PDFs I can merge?

Because processing happens directly on your device hardware, there are no artificial file limits. You can merge as many files as your device memory can handle.

Does merging PDFs reduce the quality of the documents?

No. Our digital ligase scripts perfectly stitch the structural fragments together without altering the resolution or formatting of the original pages.

Can I reorder the PDF files before merging?

Yes. Once you upload your files into the local matrix, you can drag and drop them to rearrange the exact sequence before finalizing the merge.

Does this tool work offline?

Yes. Once the web application is loaded in your browser, the client-side JavaScript operates independently of an internet connection.
