About the TIFF to PDF Tool

The PDFase TIFF to PDF tool is engineered to easily convert and merge high-resolution TIFF images into a standard, universally accessible PDF document. Whether you are archiving scanned legal documents, compiling professional photography, or sharing medical imaging, this tool ensures your structural details remain pristine.

Because everything happens directly inside your web browser, you completely skip the long upload times associated with traditional cloud converters. Your private images are entirely secure because they are never sent to or stored on external servers.

Why Name it PDFase?

In biology, an enzyme (which usually ends in "-ase") is a tiny, high-speed machine that attaches to raw materials to cut, build, or change them instantly. We applied this exact same idea to our digital tools. PDFase acts like a fast digital enzyme for your documents—modifying your files locally, instantly, and securely, without ever sending your private data over the internet.
