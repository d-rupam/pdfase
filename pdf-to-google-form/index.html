 How to Convert a PDF to a Google Form (45-Second Guide)

                
Because PDFase never asks for access to your Google account, it uses Google's own built-in Apps Script to build the form safely inside your Drive:

                
    
                    
  • Upload your PDF: Drag and drop your typed question paper into the box above, or click Select file.
    • 
                    
  • Review your questions: Check the extracted questions and options in the editor, fix any typos, and set your form title.
    • 
                    
  • Copy & Open Apps Script: Click 1. Copy Form Code, then click 2. Open Google Apps Script.
    • 
                    
  • Paste & Run: Select everything in the Google Apps Script editor, paste your code, and click the Run button at the top.
    • 
                    
  • Approve once & open your Form: On your first run, click Review Permissions → Choose your account → Advanced → Go to Untitled project → Allow. Your new Google Form will immediately appear in Google Drive!

 About the PDF to Google Form Tool

                
Manually retyping exam papers, MCQs, and homework sheets into Google Forms takes teachers hours every week. The PDFase PDF to Google Form converter extracts text locally in your browser, structures your questions and choices, and builds a ready-to-run Google Apps Script in seconds.

                
Since all processing happens on your own computer or phone, confidential exam papers are never uploaded to external servers or cloud databases.

            


            

                
 Why Name it PDFase?

                
In biology, an enzyme (which usually ends in "-ase") is a tiny, high-speed machine that attaches to raw materials to cut, build, or change them instantly. We applied this exact same idea to our digital tools. PDFase acts like a fast digital enzyme for your documents—modifying your files locally, instantly, and securely, without ever sending your private data over the internet.

            


        


        
        

            
Frequently Asked Questions

            
            

                
 Are my exam papers uploaded to a server?

                
No. The PDF to Google Form tool processes your PDF locally inside your web browser. Your question papers never leave your device.

            


            

                
 Why don't I have to sign in with Google on this site?

                
To protect your privacy and keep the tool free without API limits, PDFase never connects to your Google Drive. Instead, it gives you a clean script that you run yourself inside Google's official Apps Script editor.

            


            

                
 Why does Google show an "Unverified App" warning when I click Run?

                
Google shows that screen whenever you run a brand-new script in your own account that creates a Google Form. Since you pasted the code yourself in your own Google account, click Advanced → Go to Untitled project → Allow to let it create your form.

            


            

                
 Does it work with scanned or handwritten PDFs?

                
It works best with typed, text-based PDFs (such as question papers exported from MS Word or Google Docs). Scanned image-only PDFs do not contain selectable text for the parser to read.

            


            

                
 Can I edit the questions before creating the Google Form?

                
Yes. After selecting your PDF, you can edit any question text, add or remove multiple-choice options, switch between Multiple Choice and Short Answer, or toggle Quiz Mode right on the page.
