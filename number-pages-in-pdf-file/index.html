Step-by-Step Guide to Numbering PDF Pages Locally

Our tool is designed to be simple and fast. Follow these easy steps to add page numbers to your document:

  • Select your file: Drag and drop your PDF into the upload box above, or click the 'Select file' button to choose it from your computer or phone.
  • Configure settings: Wait a brief moment while the local engine loads the file, then choose where you want your page numbers to appear.
  • Apply numbers: Click the 'Add Page Numbers' button. Our tool will instantly overlay sequential numbering across your document.
  • Download your file: Save your newly numbered PDF document directly to your device.

About the Number Pages Tool

The PDFase Number Pages tool is designed to easily add sequential pagination to your PDF files. Whether you are preparing legal documents, organizing a research thesis, or formatting a book for print, this tool ensures your pages are perfectly ordered and easy to navigate.

Because everything happens directly inside your web browser, you completely skip the long upload times associated with traditional cloud editors. Your private files are completely secure because they are never sent to or stored on any external servers.

Why Name it PDFase?

In biology, an enzyme (which usually ends in "-ase") is a tiny, high-speed machine that attaches to raw materials to cut, build, or change them instantly. We applied this exact same idea to our digital tools. PDFase acts like a fast digital enzyme for your documents—modifying your files locally, instantly, and securely, without ever sending your private data over the internet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server when adding page numbers?

No. The Number Pages tool processes all documents locally inside your web browser. Absolute privacy is maintained, and your files never leave your device.

Is there a limit to how many pages I can number?

Because processing happens directly on your device, there are no artificial file limits. You can apply numbers to as many pages as your device memory can handle.

Does adding page numbers alter my original document layout?

No. The tool simply overlays the page numbers onto your existing document structure without shifting or altering your original text, images, or formatting.

Can I remove the page numbers later?

Once the new PDF is generated and downloaded, the page numbers become a permanent part of that specific file. We recommend keeping a copy of your original, unnumbered document just in case.

Does this tool work offline?

Yes. Once the web page is fully loaded in your browser, the tool operates entirely offline without needing an active internet connection.
