About the Bates Numbering Tool

Bates numbering is an essential indexing method used primarily in the legal, medical, and business fields to place identifying, sequential numbers on images and documents as they are processed for discovery or trial. The PDFase Bates Numbering tool is engineered specifically to streamline this workflow without the privacy risks of traditional web-based editors.

Because all logic is executed directly inside your web browser via client-side JavaScript, highly sensitive files like depositions, medical records, and corporate contracts never hit an external server. You maintain absolute control over your digital chain of custody.

Why Name it PDFase?

In biology, an enzyme (which usually ends in "-ase") is a tiny, high-speed machine that attaches to raw materials to cut, build, or change them instantly. We applied this exact same idea to our digital tools. PDFase acts like a fast digital enzyme for your documents—modifying your files locally, instantly, and securely.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server when adding Bates numbers?

No. The Bates Numbering tool processes all documents locally inside your web browser. Absolute data sovereignty is maintained, ensuring client confidentiality.

Can I add prefixes and zeros to my Bates numbers?

Yes, you can fully customize the numbering format. You can add text prefixes (e.g., 'EXHIBIT-A-'), suffixes, and define the zero-padding length (e.g., 000001).

Does stamping alter the original document layout?

No. The tool simply overlays the generated Bates stamp onto your existing document structure without shifting or altering your original text, images, or metadata.

Does this tool work offline?

Yes. Once the web page is fully loaded in your browser, the tool operates entirely offline without needing an active internet connection, perfect for secure air-gapped environments.
