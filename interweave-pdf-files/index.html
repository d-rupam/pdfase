Step-by-Step Guide to Interweaving Pages

Combining separately scanned odd and even pages is intuitive and fast. Follow these easy steps:

  • Select your files: Drag and drop multiple PDFs into the upload box above, or click 'Select files' to load them.
  • Check the order: Once loaded, ensure the file you want to draw the first page from is listed first. You can rearrange them if needed.
  • Interweave the PDFs: Click the 'Interweave PDFs' button. Our engine will pull one page from each file in alternating sequence until all pages are combined.
  • Download your file: Save your perfectly mixed, newly structured document directly to your device.

About the Interweave Tool

The PDFase Interweave tool is engineered specifically to solve the "single-sided scanner" problem. If you scanned the front sides of a document into one PDF and the back sides into another, this tool will merge them by alternating the pages, reconstructing the original document perfectly. It also effortlessly handles files of different lengths.

Because everything happens directly inside your web browser, you completely bypass the slow upload times associated with traditional cloud editors. Your private files are completely secure because they are never transmitted to any external servers.

Why Name it PDFase?

In biology, enzymes (which usually end in "-ase") are tiny, high-speed machines that attach to raw materials to cut, build, or change them instantly. We applied this exact same idea to our digital tools. PDFase acts like a fast digital enzyme for your documents—modifying your files locally, instantly, and securely, without ever sending your private data over the internet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server when interweaving them?

No. The Interweave PDF tool processes your documents locally inside your web browser. Absolute privacy is maintained, and your files never leave your device.

What exactly does interweaving PDFs mean?

It means mixing the pages of two or more documents by alternating them. For example, it takes Page 1 from File A, then Page 1 from File B, then Page 2 from File A, and so on.

What happens if one PDF has more pages than the other?

The tool perfectly alternates the pages until the shorter document runs out. After that, the remaining pages of the longer document are simply appended to the end of the new file automatically.

Can I interweave more than two files at once?

Yes! You can load multiple files. The engine will cycle through all of them, pulling one page from each in sequence before moving to the next round of pages.

Does this tool work offline?

Yes. Once the web page is fully loaded in your browser, the tool operates entirely offline without needing an active internet connection.
